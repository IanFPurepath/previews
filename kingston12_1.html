    Which patty is which?

    Fresh out of the oven they all look alike, so every patty gets stamped with coloured dots that show the filling. Point at the dots and you always get the one you want.

    No dots classic beef Red chicken Green veggie Blue beef + cheese Orange ackee & saltfish

    Spicy beef is on the menu too. Ask at the counter which stamp it gets that day.
