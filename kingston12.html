The patty key

Know your dots

Every patty comes out of the oven stamped with coloured dots. Other shops use dots for spicy or mild. At Kingston 12 they tell you the filling. Here's how to read them.

No dots classic beef Red chicken Green veggie Blue beef + cheese Orange ackee & saltfish

Colour = what's inside. Number of dots = which version. Tap any patty below to see it up close.

Spicy beef is on the menu too. Ask at the counter how it's marked that day. Dot codes as reported by blogTO; the shop would confirm before launch.
